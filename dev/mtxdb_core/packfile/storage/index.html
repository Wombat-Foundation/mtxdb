Wall-clock breakdown of one sync — the append-path sync() (dirty-scoped flush/fsync) or the full sync_all — by phase. The pack phases come from ShardPool::last_sync_split (which measures the flush and fsync legs separately) and the metadata phases from the sync method itself.
