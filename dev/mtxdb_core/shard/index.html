MAX_SHARDS

MAX_SHARD_BYTES
Maximum shard size before rotation: 2^32 - 2 bytes (~4 GiB), the largest value for which every offset a shard can ever produce still fits IndexSlot’s 32-bit offset field (which reserves its all-zero encoding as the empty-slot sentinel, capping the max representable offset at 2^32 - 2 — see index::IndexSlot). Do not round this up to a clean 4 * 1024 * 1024 * 1024: that’s one byte over the ceiling and lets a shard produce an offset IndexSlot::new panics on.
